Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

stockroom-rclone is a private tool used only by its owner to copy their own files to and from their own Google Drive.

What it accesses. Files and folders in the owner's Google Drive, through Google's Drive API, on the owner's own computers.

What it collects. Nothing. No data is sent to the maker of this tool or to any third party. Files move only between the owner's devices and the owner's Google Drive.

What it stores. A sign-in token, kept on the owner's own devices so the tool can reach the Drive. Removing the app's access in the Google account settings ends it.

Sharing. None. There are no other users, no analytics and no advertising.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
